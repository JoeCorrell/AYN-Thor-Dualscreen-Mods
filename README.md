# DualScreen Mods

Shows your Stardew Valley info on a second screen while you play.

Needs the Wemu Second Screen mod (SMAPI) running in the game.

## Today

- Date, where you are, and the clock (red after midnight)
- Day bar from 6am to 2am, with the time left
- Weather today and tomorrow
- Gold, daily luck, and what you've made or spent today
- Your hotbar and full bag, with a sort button
- Skills, with the XP left to the next level
- Your pet, but only if it needs petting or the bowl is empty
- Shipping bin total so far today
- Bar chart of the last 10 days of shipping
- Tells you when the cart is in the forest

## Map

- The game's own valley map
- Your farmer as the marker
- Location name on the game's scroll
- Indoors it just drops the marker instead of guessing

## Farm

- Crops that need water, grouped by location
- Machines, what's in them, and when they finish
- Fruit trees with fruit ready
- Animals not petted yet, and produce waiting
- Everything you can craft or cook right now

## Journal

- Quests with the current objective, not just the title
- Deadlines shown, last day in red
- Tap one for the full text, the reward, and a cancel button

## Bundles

- Community Center split by room
- Each bundle shows what's missing as item icons, plus progress
- Anything in your bag a bundle wants gets pulled to the top

## Valley

- Birthdays today and this week, with portraits and hearts
- Special orders and their deadlines
- Anyone you're carrying a loved gift for
- Who you haven't talked to today
- Rest of the season: birthdays, festivals with times and places, cart days

## Settings

- Backdrop: seasonal ground, any of the game's floor tiles, or a colour you mix
- Turn the game's art on or off
- Pick which pages show up
- Alerts on or off
- Turn off anything you don't look at, so the game skips the work
- Saved per farm, and a new farm starts from your current setup

## Controls

- Tap a hotbar slot to switch what you're holding
- Tap a bag slot to bring it to hand
- Hold a slot to eat it
- Arrows, L1/R1, or d-pad left/right to change page
- D-pad up/down to move through the hotbar

Everything runs through the game itself, so the panel always matches what the
game actually has. You can switch all of it off in the mod's config.

## Alerts

Buzzes when a machine finishes, when a birthday starts, and once at 2am. Only
on the change, so a finished machine buzzes once and then shuts up.

## Pages

Empty pages get skipped, and it remembers the page you were on even when pages
come and go.

## Finding the game

Same device by default. Set `AllowRemote` in the mod's `config.json` and it
shows up under **Found on this network**, no typing an IP.
